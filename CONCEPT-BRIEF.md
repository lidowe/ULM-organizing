# Upper Level Music — Concept Brief

One read, one veto pass. This is the backbone everything else will be built
against. If a sentence here survives your read, it becomes law for the
rebuild; if it doesn't, strike it and the build follows the strike.

---

## The concept, in one line

**You are the source.** The site is one walked signal path: it flows, it
breaks, it gets patched, it resolves — and the visitor walks it as the
signal.

## Why this concept

- It is already yours. "Start from the source", "the artist is the source",
  "the room is the first thing in the signal path", the patchbay normal —
  the metaphor is native to your copy, your trade, and your typography
  (rack legend, patch labels). We are not inventing a theme; we are
  admitting one.
- It converts the wake-up call from an accusation into a diagram. A broken
  line indicts nobody — labels, home engineers, gear makers and listeners
  are all just downstream of the break. That is your "no enemies" stance,
  drawn instead of argued.
- It gives the site a discipline it has never had: **every section either
  sits on the line or gets cut.** The "endless revisions searching for
  concept" feeling ends because there is a test.
- Nobody needs to know what a patchbay is. The line is self-explanatory;
  the trade terms are taught in single sentences where they appear — and
  teaching-in-passing is itself the brand's proof.

## The two speeds (what the research warns about)

Scroll-narrative sites fail visitors in a hurry, and two of your three
audiences are often in a hurry. So the rule: **the story is the spine, never
a gate.** On every page, at every scroll depth, the fast lane stays visible —
nav, "Work With Us Now", and the router. A visitor who gives us eight
seconds gets routed; a visitor who gives us three minutes gets moved. Both
end at the same form.

## The arc (Home, the flagship)

Act structure, each act one screen-ish, the amber line threading them:

1. **Downbeat** — the hero. Pun headline, thesis line, two actions. The
   line begins here, flowing.
2. **You, the source** — "What This Is" reframed to face the visitor: your
   project, your voice, your control. The line runs beside their story.
3. **The break** — the wake-up call, compressed to a few sentences and the
   breaking diagram. Link out to The Gap for the whole story. This is the
   only dark beat on the page, and it is short.
4. **The patch** — the three modes (hand it over / together / learn) as
   three patch points on the line. This is the services pitch, told as
   repair of the break, not as a menu.
5. **Proof** — the ribbon, the plaques, one photograph. The line runs
   through them: records the path has already carried.
6. **Resolution** — the invitation. "Tell Me What You're Working On." The
   line terminates at the form's first field.

## Page tree (lean)

- **Home** — the walked path (above).
- **The Gap** — the whole account (built; becomes the canonical "why").
- **Services** — how + costs; keeps the Q&A and Describing Sound doorways.
- **Work** — proof; credits (post-vetting) + future cases.
- **Learn** — the differentiator; grows the long-form pieces (circuit as
  emotion, why clones differ, the SM57 challenge).
- **Studio** — evidence of depth; mostly as-is.
- **About** — Edward, told once, full-strength; industry material lives in
  The Gap, not here.
- **Contact** — as-is (works), plus delivery config on the Worker.
- News/Shop/Specialists — parked until real.

## Visual language

- **The thread**: one amber line, drawn by scroll, threading Home's acts.
  Interior pages open with a short static line-mark (their position on the
  path) rather than full animation — restraint over spectacle.
- **Type stays.** Spectral / Space Grotesk / Space Mono earned their place;
  the aux tier and every-word-caps headings are law.
- **Photo roles get enforced** (evidence / index / aside, from the
  registry): one treatment per role, no more per-page invention.
- **Motion rules**: nothing moves that doesn't explain; one signature
  moment per page maximum; reduced-motion always honored; nothing blocks
  reading. The best motion disappears into the experience.
- **Red mono = clickable or a price.** Site-wide, no exceptions.

## Sound (the crown jewel, phase 4+)

A recording site that is silent is a missed signature. One opt-in audio
moment, built when you can supply clips: the same two bars through two
different circuits — opto vs FET, second- vs third-order — with the
emotional words beside them. Your thesis, audible. Nobody else in reach
does this. (Opt-in click-to-listen only; nothing autoplays, ever.)

## Voice

- Specific, wry, credentialed by detail — the coffee-to-console standard.
- "I" when Edward speaks about himself; "we" when ULM speaks about work.
- No sentence without an image in it survives ("we provide the means and
  the message" is the cautionary example).
- The wake-up call names systems, never people or companies.

## What we keep (the honestly-good list)

Type system · photo registry + responsive pipeline · contact form +
endpoint · The Gap · pre-production Q&A · Describing Sound · the router
rows · the strongest copy (coffee-to-console, Telefunken story, "a room
that lies to you", Sagan unit, "start with the problem") · deploy loop +
preview link.

## What dies

The mission told four ways (it is told once, on The Gap, and echoed in one
hero line) · sections that exist because they always have · any element
that cannot say where it sits on the line.
