Lessons & Tutoring

Train your ears. Then make cleaner calls.

For self-taught artists and engineers who can say "fix this" and want to know why the fix works, and when it won’t.

How it works

Four steps from question to progress.

  1. Free intro call — 20 minutes. We hear what you’re working on, where it’s snagged, and what you’ve already tried.
  2. A plan — You get a written sequence: what to change first, what to leave alone, and what result to expect.
  3. Sessions — 60- or 90-minute blocks, live screen-share. You drive the DAW. I coach and demonstrate.
  4. Homework with feedback — between sessions, you do real work. I review it before the next call so the next hour is targeted.

What we can cover

Pick a lane, or build a path.

  • Mixing fundamentals — gain staging, EQ, compression, the order things go in
  • Advanced mixing — buses, parallel processing, automation, mastering chains
  • Recording technique — mic choice, placement, room treatment, tracking flow
  • Production — arrangement, programming, sound design, vocal production
  • DAW fluency — Pro Tools, Logic, Ableton, or Reaper
  • Ear training — what to listen for, and how
  • Career & portfolio strategy — for people working toward this as a job
