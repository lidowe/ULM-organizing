Services

Client-led work, with room to breathe.

No preset menu. We start at the bottleneck and let the scope follow the record.

01 · Engineering

Engineering

Tracking, mixing, and mastering for artists and producers who need the record to travel cleanly from room to room.

What's included:

  • Pre-session prep — listening to references, planning signal flow
  • Up to three revision passes on mixes
  • Stems and instrumentals delivered on request
  • Master prints in the formats your distributor needs

Who it's for: artists and producers who want the work handled cleanly, with someone listening for what others miss.

02 · Production

Production

Front-to-back production from rough idea to release. Arrangement, tone, and performance are shaped until the song reads clearly.

What's included:

  • Pre-production: arrangement, key, tempo, references
  • Tracking — at my room or on location
  • Programming, sound design, additional instrumentation
  • Mix and master included or bundled with engineering

Who it's for: artists with strong ideas who need structure to turn them into something finished.

03 · Consulting

Consulting

For studios, schools, and serious home setups. We find the limiting variable first: room, monitoring, gain structure, or workflow.

What's included:

  • Room walkthrough — measurements, listening, problem ID
  • Gear chain audit: what to keep, sell, upgrade, or stop chasing
  • Workflow recommendations for DAW templates and session structure
  • Written report with prioritized next steps

Who it's for: people who have already spent money and want a clear next move instead of another purchase.

04 · Education

Education

Clinics, workshops, and curriculum design for schools, audio programs, and collectives. I teach the way I'd want to be taught: through real records, real rooms, and real problems.

Common formats:

  • Half-day or full-day clinics on a specific topic (mixing, mastering, recording drums, vocal production)
  • Multi-week curriculum design and delivery
  • Guest-lecturing for university and trade programs
  • Live mix-walkthroughs of your students' actual sessions

Who it's for: program directors, audio educators, and community-led music schools.

05 · Lessons & Tutoring

Lessons & Tutoring

One-on-one instruction for self-taught artists and engineers. The goal is better listening and better calls under pressure.

How it works:

  • Free 20-minute intro call so we can hear the project and the snag
  • Lessons in 60- or 90-minute blocks; one-off or ongoing
  • Homework with feedback between sessions — actual progress, not just talk
  • Pro Tools, Logic, Ableton, or Reaper — I work in all four

See lesson plans & book

06 · Used Gear

Used Gear

Gear rotates through my own room first. Listings note real condition, known limits, and what the piece is actually good for.

How it works:

  • Current listings on the gear page — refreshed when new pieces come in
  • Each item has full condition notes, original photos, and known issues if any
  • Local pickup or shipped insured
  • 30-day functional return on anything I sold as "working"

Looking for something specific? Tell me — sometimes I have it on the shelf, sometimes I can source it.
