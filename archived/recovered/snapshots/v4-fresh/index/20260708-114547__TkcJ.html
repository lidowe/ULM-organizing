Ways to work

Choose the role you need me to play in this project.

Most projects combine these roles at different stages. Scope is built from your workflow, not from a template package.

Producer / Executive Producer

Creative direction from demo to final master.

Arrangement, performance priorities, session planning, and release-level decisions for artists who need strong project leadership.

Engineering / Mix

Tracking, comping, editing, mix prep, and mixing.

Recording and mix services for bands, solo artists, and producer-led sessions that need clean execution and clear revisions.

Co-Engineer / Coaching

Share technical load without losing autonomy.

Support for existing engineers and artists: vocal coaching, session troubleshooting, workflow optimization, and structured mentorship.

Session flow

Real studio stages, clear technical handoffs.

Projects move faster when each stage has a defined output and file standard.

1. Pre-Production

Define arrangement, references, session plan, and performance targets before recording starts.

2. Tracking + Overdubs

Capture core takes, then build overdubs with role clarity and take management.

3. Comping + Edit + Mix Prep

Consolidate performances, timing, tuning, and session organization before mix decisions.

4. Mix + Mastering Coordination

Run mix revisions, translation checks, and final master handoff with delivery specs.
