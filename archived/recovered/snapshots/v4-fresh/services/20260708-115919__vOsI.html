Services

How support enters a record.

Some records need full producer leadership. Others need focused engineering, vocal production, or collaborative technical support around an existing team.

Scope is built from the material

Scope is defined from the current stage, the available files, and the desired sonic destination. No fixed package language required.

Producer / Executive Producer

Creative and project leadership

  • Song and arrangement development from demo stage
  • Session planning, personnel direction, and priorities
  • Performance direction across tracking days
  • Final decision support through mix and master approval

Used when the project benefits from one leadership thread through arrangement, session decisions, and final approvals.

Recording / Mix Engineering

Technical execution

  • Tracking sessions, overdubs, and punch-in management
  • Vocal comping, tuning passes, and edit cleanup
  • Mix prep with routing, gain staging, and session organization
  • Mixing with structured revision rounds

Used when source material exists and the record needs disciplined execution from session files to final mix revisions.

Vocal Production / Coaching

Performance and vocal direction

  • Coaching for artists unfamiliar with studio workflow
  • Take strategy, doubles, harmonies, and vocal arrangement
  • Session pacing to reduce fatigue and over-recording
  • Performance decisions tied to mix translation

Used when vocal performance and delivery need an experienced room partner without changing the artist's identity.

Co-Engineer Support / Mentorship

Share the technical load

  • Support for active engineers needing a second technical brain
  • Remote session troubleshooting and decision review
  • Home-studio workflow optimization and gear strategy
  • Structured mentorship for engineers and producer-artists

Used when engineers and producer-artists want to keep control while adding technical continuity and decision confidence.

Engagement formats

Built to match schedule, budget, and release cadence.

Per-song or per-project

Common for mixing, vocal production, and targeted production support.

Session-day blocks

Common for tracking, overdubs, and in-person production days.

Retained technical support

Common for artists, teams, and engineers handling recurring releases.
