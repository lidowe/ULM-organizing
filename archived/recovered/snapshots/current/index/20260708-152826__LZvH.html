About

You lead the record. I keep the room honest.

I’m not here to overwrite your intent. I’m here to make the technical side behave like a professional room, so your choices land the way you meant them to.

Most clients ask specific questions: "fix this vocal," "why does this fall apart in the car," "what should change first?" The job is precise answers with measurable consequences.
