Studio

Private by appointment.

Upper Level Music is a focused recording, mix, production, and signal-flow environment in Columbia, South Carolina.

The room is not advertised as a gear museum. It is built as a working path from performance to record.

The studio is suited to vocals, overdubs, production work, hybrid mixing, mastering prep, private consulting, and technical system work. The value is not only the equipment list; it is the way the path is chosen, wired, gained, monitored, and revised for the record.

Selected analog tools, custom cabling, patchbay logic, microphone choices, preamp character, dynamics, EQ, conversion, and monitoring are treated as one system. That makes the room flexible without becoming arbitrary.

For projects that need larger tracking rooms, live drums, or a different acoustic scale, the work can be planned around appropriate outside rooms and brought back into the Upper Level Music workflow.

Tracking

Source-first capture

Microphone and chain decisions are made around the voice, instrument, arrangement, and emotional target of the song.

Mix

Hybrid workflow

Analog and digital tools are used where they help the record, not to prove a format preference.

Systems
