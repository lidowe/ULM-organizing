Services & rates

Tell me where the record is stuck.

You can book one stage, bring in a specific problem, or scope a project across production, recording, editing, mixing, and final delivery.

Recording & tracking

Focused source capture, overdubs, vocals, instruments, and session engineering.

Chain, room, and session preparation are based on the source and the goal of the record rather than a standard preset workflow.

$65–$100 / hrday rates from $500

Vocal production & editing

Performance direction, comping, tuning to taste, timing, vocal editing, and preparation for mix.

Scope changes significantly between a clean comp and a dense modern vocal production, so the range reflects the work involved.

$150–$400 / songbased on scope

Mixing

Hybrid mixing with the production and emotional hierarchy of the song kept in view from the first balance through final delivery.

Track count, editing requirements, production involvement, alternate versions, and turnaround affect the final quote.

$400–$900 / songtypical independent range

Mastering

Final tone, level, consistency, sequencing perspective, and release-ready delivery.

Album or EP projects are quoted as a body of work when that makes more sense than per-song pricing.

$100–$175 / songrelease packages quoted

Production & arrangement

Song development, arrangement, parts, instrumentation, programming, and carrying a rough idea into a finished production.

This can be one song or an ongoing role across a project. The shape of the assignment matters more than an hourly number.

Full-project development

Consultation & studio systems

Targeted help for artists, producers, engineers, and private studios: session strategy, mix diagnosis, signal flow, routing, analog integration, wiring, gain structure, and technical troubleshooting.

$100–$150 / hrremote or by appointment

Rates are a starting point, not a judgment on the project.

Independent budgets are real. Where the scope allows, I work on a sliding scale to get closer to an artist's actual budget without pretending every assignment requires the same amount of work.

Unusual or passion-driven projects may be quoted below standard rates when added creative freedom changes the nature of the assignment. Complex, multi-tier projects are scoped personally before work begins.
