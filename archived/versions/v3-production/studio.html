The room

Built for decisions, not foot traffic.

Upper Level Music is a private studio rather than a high-volume commercial facility. Sessions are by appointment so the room can be prepared around the work in front of it.

The studio combines a deep microphone locker, flexible analog processing and routing, hybrid mixing, and a modern digital workflow. The useful part is not the quantity of equipment. It is the ability to change the path when the song, voice, or problem calls for something different.

For artists

You can talk about the song in human terms.

You do not need to arrive with a technical plan or know what processor should solve a problem. Tell me what feels wrong, what you want the listener to feel, and what you are trying not to lose.

For producers & engineers

The technical depth is there when you need it.

If the problem is gain structure, routing, mic/preamp interaction, analog integration, summing, stereo behavior, conversion, or a system that refuses to behave as expected, we can work directly at that level without turning it into a beginner lesson.

Selected technical range

Available, not advertised as the point.

For clients who care about the details, a small view into the studio's range. This is not a complete equipment list.

Microphones & front end
A deep microphone locker across condenser, tube, FET, ribbon, and dynamic designs; multiple preamp topologies and impedance behaviors; flexible source-to-front-end pairing rather than a single default vocal chain.
Analog processing & summing
Multiple EQ and dynamics topologies, transformer and transformerless paths, parallel and serial routing, analog summing, and the ability to change order and interaction rather than forcing every source through one fixed chain.
Conversion, routing & system design
High-headroom conversion, extensive patching and routing, custom cabling and signal-flow work, and a studio architecture designed to make comparison and problem isolation fast.
