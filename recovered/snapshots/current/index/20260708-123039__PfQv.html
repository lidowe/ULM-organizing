About

You lead. I support the record.

I do not direct your artistic identity. I operate as the technical layer: the human version of major-label studio standards, applied to your direction.

Most clients ask specific questions: "fix this vocal," "why does this collapse in the car," "what should I change first?" The job is precise answers with measurable consequences.
