About

Built in real rooms.

Edward Lidow is a recording engineer, mixer, producer, and studio systems designer based in Columbia, South Carolina.

A record has to survive both scrutiny and instinct. The technical work is there to protect the performance, not replace it.

Edward’s foundation came through formal training at SAE Institute Miami followed by twelve years inside Hit Factory / Criteria Miami. That environment demanded speed, recall, discretion, and accurate decisions under real session pressure.

Across that period and the years that followed, the work touched hip-hop, pop, Latin, rock, country, independent records, commercial sessions, and technical studio systems. The common thread is not genre. It is knowing how to move from artist intention to a finished recording without losing the human part of the performance.

Upper Level Music is the current expression of that work: a private studio and professional service platform for recording, mixing, vocal production, production, mastering, and signal-flow consultation.
