# Upper Level Music - Strategy Brief V1

Date: 2026-07-08
Purpose: Lock brand, audience, offers, and site behavior before visual or copy redesign.

## 1) Core Positioning (Locked)
Upper Level Music is a high-credibility technical artist practice that translates client intent into release-ready work without forcing the client into a template.

Operational interpretation:
- Client intent is the constraint.
- Technical decisions are creative decisions.
- Deliverables can be done-for-you or learn-with-you, but both protect the same artistic center.

## 2) What Makes This Different (Locked)
Most operators are either:
- Narrow specialists (mix only, tracking only), or
- Content-driven generalists with shallow technical depth.

Upper Level Music differentiator:
- Deep major-room engineering discipline plus modern independent flexibility.
- Can execute, consult, or teach from the same technical foundation.
- Can translate vague artistic language into concrete production and engineering moves.

## 3) Non-Negotiables (Locked)
- No sponsored-influence behavior that distorts recommendations.
- No technical choice made for trend signaling alone.
- No client steering that trades artistic intent for easy market mimicry.

## 4) Audience Model (Locked)
Primary fit clients:
- Artists who need production/mix/finishing execution and want intent preserved.
- Artist-engineers/producers who need advanced guidance and rigorous feedback.
- Creators stuck between idea and translation (record does not sound like the intent).

Lower-fit clients:
- People seeking credibility extraction with no long-term trust relationship.
- People shopping for authority theater instead of real work.

Common stated request:
- "I need a mix" / "I need this to sound professional" / "I am stuck."

Common root problem:
- Weak decision system (arrangement, capture, tone, workflow) caused by fragmented learning and poor preparation discipline.

## 5) Service System (Locked)
Do not present as random broad services. Present as a 2-lane operating model.

Lane A: Done-for-you
- Mixes, production, editing, vocal work, finishing.
- Outcome: release-ready deliverables.

Lane B: Learn-with-you
- Mentorship, diagnostic feedback, system building, workflow and equipment optimization.
- Outcome: client can make better decisions independently.

Bridge principle:
- Even in done-for-you projects, client understanding improves.
- Even in education projects, real deliverable progress occurs.

## 6) Site Conversion Model (Locked)
Primary CTA label: Tell Your Story

Primary conversion action:
- Structured inquiry describing current stage, blockers, and desired outcome.

Secondary conversion actions:
- Request a mix review.
- Apply for mentorship/technical guidance.

CTA logic:
- Do not force users into rigid package selection before diagnosis.
- Intake captures signal, then service path is prescribed.

## 7) Credibility Placement (Locked)
Homepage: restrained proof only.
- One concise line on high-level credentials (Grammy/platinum/chart involvement).
- Link to full proof on Work page.

Work page: full evidence.
- Detailed credits, artist list, rooms, role disclaimer.

## 8) Voice and Language Rules (Locked)
Voice target:
- Precise
- Artist-first
- Technically literate
- Direct
- Non-performative

Language bans:
- Avoid vague, unfalsifiable adjectives and hype language.
- Avoid metaphor overload that hides real process.
- Avoid generic AI phrases about authenticity/spirit without operational meaning.

Preferred sentence pattern:
- Problem -> decision -> consequence.

Examples:
- Good: "If the arrangement is crowded, no mix trick will recover vocal authority. We solve arrangement first, then process tone."
- Bad: "We make your sound shine and stay true to your soul."

## 9) Homepage Architecture (V1)
Order of sections:
1. Hero: who this is for + what happens here.
2. Two service lanes: Done-for-you and Learn-with-you.
3. "How work starts": Tell Your Story intake.
4. "How decisions are made": technical-as-art framework.
5. Restrained credibility strip + link to Work.
6. Final CTA.

Hero objective:
- State mechanism, not mood.
- Remove ambiguity about who should inquire.

## 10) Contact/Intake Requirements (V1)
Inquiry form should collect:
- Role (artist, producer, engineer, other)
- Current stage (idea/demo/rough mix/near-finish/release prep)
- What is blocking progress now
- Desired outcome (done-for-you vs guidance vs both)
- Timeline and budget band
- References (2-3 tracks)

Routing rule:
- Form responses should support triage into Lane A or Lane B quickly.

## 11) 90-Day Success Criteria (Initial)
Current target is directional, because baseline is not yet measured.

Initial KPI proposal:
- 12+ qualified inquiries
- 6+ discovery calls or structured follow-ups
- 3+ paid projects started
- 2+ mentorship clients retained for at least 1 month

Quality KPI:
- At least 70% of inquiries clearly match one lane after first response.
