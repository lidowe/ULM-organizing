About

Edward Lidow.

Engineer, mixer, producer, studio designer, and educator. Formed in the large-room studio system; now working independently through Upper Level Music.

Edward Lidow’s foundation came through formal training at SAE Institute Miami and twelve years at Hit Factory / Criteria Miami, a room culture built around large-format consoles, serious clients, disciplined signal flow, and the apprenticeship model that once formed many working engineers.

That background matters because it was not abstract training. It was daily exposure to records being made under pressure: artists, producers, labels, vocal sessions, technical problems, late revisions, and the difference between sounding good in the room and surviving as a finished record.

Upper Level Music is the current expression of that background: private recording, mixing, vocal production, production support, and studio systems work in Columbia, South Carolina. The work is intentionally personal, by appointment, and built around the record rather than the room’s ego.

Public name

Edward Lidow is the primary public-facing name. Some release metadata may also appear under Ed Lidow, Ed Lido, or Jewfro.

The advertised studio brand is Upper Level Music.

The job is not to make the equipment impressive. The job is to make the artist more believable, the record more intentional, and the technical path disappear into the feeling.
