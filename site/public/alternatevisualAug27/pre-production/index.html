Getting started

A Chance to Be Heard.

Before we reach for a microphone, a short, informal Q&A. We wouldn’t know which mic to start with without knowing what’s being delivered into it.

01Why we ask

Because we’ve been on both sides of the process, we know how frustrating it is to be told how to think and what to say to make a ‘hit’. Maybe some people welcome that. For us, the source is the guide, and we work in service of the song or project in front of us — not building your ‘brand’ or ‘image’. That reveals itself through collaboration, in the process.

A typical Q&A might go like this — but we try not to be typical. The conversation establishes a few constants we’ve learned are necessary, and the rest is a chance to be heard before we reach for a microphone.

So let’s find out:

02A sample of the conversation

What are you making, and where does it live right now?

A single, an EP, a demo that outgrew the bedroom. “Three songs on a laptop and one that only exists live” is a complete answer.

What is it supposed to feel like?

Not the genre — the feeling. “Like driving at night.” “Dreamy, like we’re floating in space.” References, adjectives and colors all count. Translating them is our job, not yours.

What made you write it?

This is the one that matters. The motivation behind a project at the beginning is how the message survives to the end with your voice intact.

What is actually getting in the way?

The vocal never sits. The low end disappears in the car. It has been eighty percent finished for a year. Say it plainly — it is usually fixable, and it is never embarrassing.

How much of it do you want in your own hands?

Handed over, worked through together, or taught. There is no wrong answer, and it can change mid-project.

When does it need to exist, and what does “done” look like?

A release date, a playlist pitch, a master for vinyl, or just finally finished. The destination sets the route.

The point of all of it: the pressure gets dealt with here, ahead of time, so when creativity is flowing nothing interrupts it.
